Last summer I had the pleasure of co-facilitating a discussion on tracking design system deviations at one of my favorite design system events, [The Question](https://bencallahan.com/the-question). Ben Callahan wrote a [fantastic recap](https://bencallahan.com/design-system-deviation-is-a-signal) of the conversation, with the key takeaway being that design system deviations are a _signal_ of potential feature gaps, _not_ necessarily a violation that must be "corrected".
